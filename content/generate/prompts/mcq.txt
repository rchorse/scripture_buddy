RULE 1 — verse_refs must be verses in THIS chapter.
Use the exact form "{chapter_ref}:N" where N is one of the verse numbers shown below. Never cite another chapter, another book, or a verse number that does not appear in the list below. Cite the verse or verses the answer actually comes from.

RULE 2 — the four choices must be distinct.
No two choices may say the same thing in different words. Exactly one must be correct, and answer_index must point to it (0-based).

Content guidance:
- Questions must be answerable from the chapter text alone. No outside doctrine, commentary, or interpretation beyond what the text plainly states.

- Do not make the correct answer identifiable by style alone — avoid making it noticeably longer, more detailed, or more scriptural-sounding than the others.

- Vary what you test across the {n} questions: people, actions, sequence of events, stated reasons, places.
